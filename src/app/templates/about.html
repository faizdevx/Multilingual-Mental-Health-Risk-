About

What this is

A research / portfolio NLP system that classifies sentiment, emotion and stress-related language in text and reports calibrated model probabilities and model uncertainty. It is a screening / research classifier.

It is not a diagnostic tool. It does not diagnose depression, anxiety, PTSD or any disorder, cannot tell whether someone has a mental illness, does not predict suicide risk, and must not replace a clinician.

What the "risk" output actually is

The risk head is trained on Dreaddit (English Reddit posts from stress-related communities, crowd-annotated for stress). Its label is stress / non_stress. No dataset used here defines suicide-risk or severity levels, so the system never outputs such categories. The probability is a statistical model output (temperature-scaled on a validation split) and is not a clinical probability.

Multilingual support is limited to the evaluated languages. Risk is evaluated on English only; for Hindi / Hinglish the risk output is an unvalidated zero-shot transfer.

Datasets (separate populations, never merged into one label space)

  • Risk: Dreaddit (Turcan & McKeown, 2019)
  • Sentiment: Tweet Sentiment Multilingual (Barbieri et al., 2022; English + Romanized-Hindi tweets) and a Hinglish YouTube-comment corpus
  • Emotion: BRIGHTER / SemEval-2025 Task 11 (English + Hindi)

Privacy

Submitted text is processed in memory, never logged, printed or stored. Logs hold only request ID, timestamp, route, status, latency and model version.

If you or someone else may be in distress

This tool cannot help with that. Please contact a qualified professional or local emergency/crisis service. International directories (choose your own country): findahelpline.com · IASP crisis centres.
